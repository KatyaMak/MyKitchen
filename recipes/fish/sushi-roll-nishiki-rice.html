Sushi Roll — Nishiki Rice Edition

stovetop  ·  45 minutes  ·  makes 3 rolls, serves 2  ·  ★★★★★
Nishiki rice — ½ cup water — 1 cup rice vinegar — 1 tbsp sugar — ½ tsp salt — ¼ tsp nori sheets crab stick avocado cucumber Brie, thin slices, rind off — if using ✦ gourmaise — tiny drizzle sesame seeds
Start here
Rice first — everything depends on it
Measure ½ cup Nishiki. Rinse 3–4 times until the water runs almost clear. Sushi rice wants a tighter ratio than risotto — 1 cup water to the ½ cup rice. Bring to a boil, cover, drop to the lowest heat, and cook 12 minutes. Off heat, lid still on, 10 minutes more. Don't peek — every lift of the lid lets out steam the rice still needs.
Step 1 — while the rice rests
Mix the seasoning
Stir together 1 tbsp rice vinegar, ½ tsp sugar, ¼ tsp salt. Stir until the sugar and salt fully dissolve, and have it ready before the rice finishes — it goes in warm.
Step 2
Season and cool the rice
When the rice is done, fold the vinegar mix in gently while it's still warm — fan it a little as you fold. Let it cool to just above room temperature before rolling. Warm rice tears the nori and won't hold a roll.
Step 3
Set up the roll
Nori shiny side down, shorter side toward you. Wet your fingers and spread the rice in a thin, even layer, leaving a 2cm gap at the far edge — that bare strip is what seals the roll. Lay the fillings in a strip about ⅓ of the way up from the bottom edge: crab stick, avocado, cucumber, thin slices of Brie with the rind off if using, and a tiny drizzle of gourmaise.
Finish
Roll, seed, slice
Roll firmly away from you, tucking tight as you go, and press to hold at the seal strip. Sprinkle sesame seeds on the outside after rolling and press gently so they stick. Wet the knife between cuts and use single clean strokes — 6–8 pieces per roll.
Makes 3 rolls — plenty for two, with one left over. 🍣
