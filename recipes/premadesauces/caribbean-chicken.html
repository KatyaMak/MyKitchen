Caribbean chicken tenderloins with pineapple & shrimp

April 21, 2026  ·  stovetop  ·  20 minutes  ·  serves 2  ·  ★★★★★
chicken tenderloins ~250g shrimp — handful, thawed and patted dry fresh pineapple — chunks 2–3cm Caribbean sauce — Lunds & Byerlys, generous pour ✦ ground nutmeg — pinch off heat salt, pepper, oil jasmine rice — alongside
Start here — 18 min before serving
Rice first
1 cup jasmine rice, rinsed. 1.5 cups water, pinch of salt, knob of butter. Bring to boil, immediately lowest simmer, lid on, 18 minutes. Do not lift lid. Off heat, 5 more minutes. Fluff. Everything else fits inside those 18 minutes.
Step 1 — 6–8 min
Sear the chicken
Pat tenderloins dry, salt and pepper. Medium-high heat, oil shimmering. In — don't crowd, don't touch. 3–4 minutes first side until golden. Flip, 2–3 minutes. Out onto a plate to rest.
Step 2 — 3–4 min
Caramelize the pineapple
Same pan, same heat. Pineapple chunks in — single layer, don't stir immediately. Let them sit and go golden one side, 2 minutes. Toss once. They should look caramelized and smell incredible.
Step 3 — 2 min
Sauce in
Caribbean sauce in — generous, enough to coat everything. Stir, bubble and come together 1–2 minutes. Already fully seasoned — taste before adding any salt.
Step 4 — 2 min
Shrimp in
Shrimp in — they go pink and curl, that's done. 2 minutes maximum. Don't overcook.
Step 5 — 1 min
Chicken back in
Tenderloins back in, toss gently to coat. Warm through 1 minute — already cooked, don't overdo it.
Finish
Serve
Off heat — pinch of ground nutmeg, stir once. Over jasmine rice, sauce poured over everything. 🍍
Tired weeknight, overripe pineapple, frostbitten shrimp, nutmeg "ready to go to school" — still amazing. Nutmeg off heat: quiet warm magic. 🌴
Pre-made sauce philosophy: when the sauce is already perfect, trust it. Add texture (pineapple), add protein (shrimp), add one background spice (nutmeg). Don't compete with it.
