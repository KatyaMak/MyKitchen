Dutch oven beef stew

May 2026  ·  stovetop + oven · Dutch oven  ·  serves 2–3  ·  ★★★★★
beef stew chunks — pat very dry red wine ~150–200ml tomatoes × 2, roughly chopped onion × 1 medium garlic — 4–5 cloves, smashed carrots × 2–3, chunky coins potatoes — chunky 3–4cm sweet potatoes — chunky 3–4cm chicken or beef broth ~300ml smoked paprika · thyme · bay leaves × 2 whole cloves × 3 ✦ whole allspice × 5 ✦ black peppercorns × 8 ✦ olive oil
Start here
Oven on — 160°C / 325°F
Preheat while you prep everything. Pat beef chunks completely dry with paper towel — every piece. Salt and pepper generously on all sides. Dry meat sears. Wet meat steams.

Prep: onion — medium dice. Carrots — chunky coins, ~1.5cm. Garlic — 4–5 cloves smashed, skin off. Tomatoes — roughly chopped. Potatoes and sweet potatoes — chunky 3–4cm pieces, kept separate. They go in late.
Step 1 — 10–12 min
Sear the beef
Dutch oven, medium-high heat, 2 tbsp olive oil. Hot before the meat goes in — oil should shimmer.

Beef in single layer with space between pieces — do in 2 batches if needed. Do not crowd or they steam. Do not touch for 3 minutes. Flip when deeply brown — not golden, brown. 2–3 minutes other side.

Out onto a plate. Repeat second batch. Leave all the fat and dark bits in the pot — that's the flavor base.
Step 2 — 8 min
Build the base
Same pot, reduce to medium heat. Onion in — stir, soften 4 minutes until translucent and going golden at edges. Garlic in — stir 1 minute. Carrots in — stir 2 minutes. Smoked paprika 1 tsp — stir 30 seconds, let it bloom in the fat. It will smell immediately wonderful.
Step 3 — 3 min
Wine
Pour in 150–200ml red wine — it will bubble dramatically. Scrape the bottom with a wooden spoon — every dark bit comes up and becomes sauce. Let it reduce 2–3 minutes until the sharp alcohol smell softens and it looks like a loose dark syrup.
Step 4
Tomatoes, broth, herbs and whole spices
Tomatoes in — roughly crush with spoon as they go. Pour in 300ml broth. Liquid should come about ¾ up the beef — not submerged, not dry.

Now add: bay leaves × 2, thyme — good pinch dried, whole cloves × 3, whole allspice × 5, black peppercorns × 8.

Stir. Beef back in — nestle into the liquid. Any resting juices from the plate go in too.
Step 5 — 1 hour
Into the oven
Bring to a gentle bubble on the stovetop. Lid on. Into the oven at 160°C / 325°F.

Timer 1 hour. Walk away. Pour yourself some of that wine. 🍷
Step 6 — 35 min more
Potatoes and sweet potatoes in
At 1 hour — open, check liquid (add small splash of broth if it looks dry), taste.

Potatoes and sweet potatoes in — push gently into the liquid. Fine if some peek above — they'll steam from the heat and the lid traps everything. Lid back on. Back in the oven.

Timer 35 minutes.
Finish
Serve
Out of the oven. Fish out bay leaves, cloves, allspice, peppercorns. Taste — adjust salt.

The broth should be dark, silky, deeply flavored. Sweet potatoes will have partially dissolved and thickened everything naturally — that's the direction to let happen.

Drizzle of good olive oil. Serve straight from the Dutch oven into deep bowls. Bread alongside — non negotiable. 🥖
Red wine, whole spices, root vegetables, low and slow — this is exactly what the Dutch oven was made for. Whole cloves, allspice and peppercorns go in with the liquid and work quietly for two hours. The broth ends up with unusual depth — the kind where people ask what you did differently. 🍷
