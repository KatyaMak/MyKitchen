White beans and sausage braise

April 20, 2026  ·  stovetop · Dutch oven  ·  serves 2  ·  ★★★★★
Johnsonville Brown Sugar Honey sausages × 8 small links white beans — 1.5 cups soaked overnight 1 medium tomato, roughly chopped onion × 1 medium garlic — 3–4 cloves carrots × 2, chunky coins chicken broth ~400ml white wine ~60ml smoked paprika · dried rosemary · coriander · red pepper flakes bay leaves × 2 whole cloves × 3 ✦ olive oil
Night before
Soak the beans
1.5 cups white beans in plenty of cold water, overnight. Drain and rinse before using.
Step 1 — 5–6 min
Brown the sausages
Medium-high heat, Dutch oven, tiny splash of oil. Sausages in — don't touch 2–3 minutes, let them get color. Turn, another 2 minutes. The sugar in these sausages caramelizes slightly — exactly what you want. They don't need to cook through, just golden. Out onto a plate. Leave all the fat in the pot.
Step 2 — 8 min
Build the base
Same pot, medium heat. Onion in — soften 4 minutes. Garlic in — 1 minute. Carrots in — stir 2 minutes. Smoked paprika, coriander, rosemary (crumble between fingers first), red pepper flakes in now — stir 30 seconds, let them bloom in the fat.
Step 3 — 4 min
Wine and tomato
Pour in 60ml white wine — scrape the bottom, all that caramelized sausage flavor comes up. Reduce 1–2 minutes. Tomato in, roughly crush with spoon. Stir, let it come together 2 minutes.
Step 4 — 40 min
Beans and broth
Soaked beans in, stir to coat in everything. Pour over 400ml chicken broth — liquid should just cover the beans, add splash more if needed. Bay leaves and whole cloves × 3 in. Nestle sausages back on top. Bring to a gentle boil, then immediately down to low simmer. Lid on. Timer 40 minutes. Check once at 20 minutes — stir gently, check liquid.

At 40 min — beans should be tender and creamy, broth silky and reduced. If beans still have bite, 10 more minutes lid on.
Finish
Serve
Fish out bay leaves and whole cloves. Taste — adjust salt. Drizzle of good olive oil over the top. Serve straight from Dutch oven into deep bowls. Bread alongside — non negotiable. 🥖
Brown Sugar Honey sausages were a happy accident — their sweetness played beautifully against the savory beans and rosemary. 3 whole cloves was exactly right: quiet dark warmth underneath, you won't name it as clove, you'll just notice the broth has unusual depth. A French farmhouse kitchen on a Sunday afternoon kind of dish. 🍷
Next time: consider more tomato for brighter acidity. The braise was wonderful but slightly clear — one more tomato would push it in the right direction.
