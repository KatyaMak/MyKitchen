Green and yellow pea braise with bacon and sausages

April 22, 2026  ·  stovetop · Dutch oven  ·  serves 2  ·  ★★★★★
green and yellow peas — 1.5 cups mixed, soaked overnight bacon strips × 4 — cut into lardons small sausages × 7 + Boar's Head sausages — two kinds ✦ onion × 1 medium garlic — 3–4 cloves carrots × 2, chunky coins 1 medium tomato, roughly chopped chicken broth ~400ml white wine ~60ml smoked paprika · rosemary · coriander · red pepper flakes bay leaves × 2 whole cloves × 2 ✦ olive oil
Night before
Soak the peas
1.5 cups mixed green and yellow peas in plenty of cold water, overnight. Drain and rinse before using.
Step 1 — 5 min
Render the bacon
Cold Dutch oven, no oil. Bacon lardons in, medium heat. Let fat render slowly — stir occasionally. You want crispy golden lardons and a good pool of fat. This is your flavor base. Out onto a plate, leave all the fat.
Step 2 — 5 min
Brown the sausages
Same fat, medium-high heat. Sausages in — don't touch 2–3 minutes, get color. Turn, 2 more minutes. Out onto same plate as bacon. Leave the fat.
Step 3 — 8 min
Build the base
Medium heat. Onion in — soften 4 minutes. Garlic in — 1 minute. Carrots in — stir 2 minutes. Smoked paprika, coriander, rosemary, red pepper flakes — stir 30 seconds, bloom in the fat.
Step 4 — 3 min
Wine and tomato
White wine in — scrape the bottom. Reduce 1–2 minutes. Tomato in, crush with spoon, stir 1 minute.
Step 5 — 40 min
Peas and broth
Drained rinsed peas in, stir to coat. Pour over 400ml chicken broth — just cover the peas, add splash more if needed. Bay leaves and whole cloves × 2 in. Bacon and sausages nestled back on top. Gentle boil, then immediately low simmer. Lid on. 40 minutes. Check once at 20 min.

At 40 min — peas tender and creamy, broth silky. If still bite — 10 more minutes.
Finish
Serve
Fish out bay leaves and cloves. Taste — go easy on salt, bacon and sausages are already salty. Drizzle of olive oil. Deep bowls, bread alongside. 🥖
Two kinds of sausage — the decision made itself and was correct. Whole cloves again, becoming a signature. Green and yellow peas beautiful together — sweet, earthy, colorful. The white bean magic but lighter. 🥘
