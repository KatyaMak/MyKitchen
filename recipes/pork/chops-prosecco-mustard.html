Pork chops with Prosecco-mustard cream sauce

April 1, 2026  ·  stovetop + oven  ·  serves 3  ·  ★★★★★
pork loin chops — boneless center cut Dijon mustard Fage yogurt Prosecco ~60ml chicken broth ~60ml sweet potatoes baby spinach smoked paprika thyme garlic lemon butter
Start here
Oven on — 200°C / 400°F
Sweet potatoes go in first — they need the most time.
Step 1 — 35–40 min
Sweet potatoes into the oven
Cube sweet potatoes into similar-sized pieces so they cook evenly. Toss with olive oil, salt, smoked paprika, dried thyme, and 2 smashed garlic cloves. Single layer on a baking sheet — do not pile them up. Into the oven. Set a timer for 35–40 minutes. Forget them completely. They will be fine.
Step 2
The pork chops — no beating needed
These are center cut boneless loin chops, already uniform. No need to beat them — that's for schnitzel. Score the fat edge with 2–3 small cuts so the chop doesn't curl up in the pan. Pat the chops completely dry with paper towel — wet meat doesn't sear, it steams. Salt and pepper both sides generously.
Step 3 — 3–4 min per side
Searing
Big pan, loose — at least 2–3cm between each chop. If they're crowded they steam instead of sear. If the pan isn't big enough for all three: do two first, rest them, then the third.

Medium-high heat, small splash of oil. When the oil shimmers — chops in. Do not touch them. 3–4 minutes per side until genuinely golden. Check with thermometer: 63°C / 145°F. Pull immediately.
Step 4 — 5 min, non-negotiable
Rest
Normal plate. Loosely tent with aluminum foil — fold a piece and lay it gently over the top, not wrapped tight. You want warmth in but a little steam out so the crust stays. 5 minutes. Do not cut. Do not peek. The juices need to settle.
Step 5 — same pan, do not wash it
The sauce
That's your flavor base on the bottom. Medium heat. Pour in ~60ml Prosecco — it will bubble dramatically. Scrape the bottom with a wooden spoon, all that color comes up. Let it reduce about 1 minute. Add ~60ml chicken broth. Reduce another minute.

One generous spoonful of Dijon mustard, stir. Two spoonfuls of Fage, stir gently. Salt, pepper, taste. It should coat a spoon but stay pourable. Pour into a small cup or mug to keep warm.
Step 6 — last 3 minutes, same pan
Spinach
Don't wash the pan. Knob of butter, optional garlic. Half a bag of baby spinach (~70g for three — it wilts down dramatically). Toss until just wilted. Squeeze of lemon. Done.
Plate
Bring it together
Chops whole or sliced, sauce poured over. Sweet potatoes alongside. Spinach next to it, still green and alive.
Pour yourself something. You did well. 🥂
