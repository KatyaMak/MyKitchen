Sage butter chicken with parmesan mash

April 2026  ·  stovetop  ·  serves 2 + leftovers  ·  ~45 min  ·  ★★★★★
chicken tenderloins ~250g Yukon Gold potatoes — 6–7 medium baby bella mushrooms — whole, slice before cooking zucchini × 1 small yellow squash × 1 small butter — generous, used several times Fage yogurt — 2–3 tbsp for mash milk — small splash, warm Parmesan — for mash and finishing fresh sage — 6–8 leaves fresh thyme — few sprigs fresh oregano — finishing, raw ✦ (don't forget) garlic — 2 cloves, lightly smashed olive oil, salt, pepper
Start here — 20–25 min
Potatoes on first
Peel and cube potatoes into roughly equal pieces. Cold salted water, bring to boil, simmer until completely tender — a knife goes in with zero resistance, 20–25 minutes. Don't rush this. Everything else fits inside this window.
Step 1 — 8 min · while potatoes cook
Mushrooms — properly
Slice baby bellas about 5mm thick — not too thin. Wide pan, medium-high heat, small splash of oil. In — single layer, do not crowd, do not touch for 2 minutes. Let them brown properly on one side first. Toss, another 2 minutes. Salt only after they have color — salt early draws moisture and they steam instead of brown. Strip fresh thyme directly over them while they cook. Toss once more. Out onto a plate, cover loosely.
The rules: high heat, space, patience. No touching. No crowding. No early salt. Break any of these and they steam into sad grey things.
Step 2 — 5 min
Zucchini and squash
Same pan, same heat. Slice into half-moons about 1cm thick. Little oil. Same logic — don't crowd, let them color. 2 minutes each side. Salt, pepper. Out onto the same plate as the mushrooms. Cover loosely — plate or lid slightly ajar, not sealed tight or they go soggy.
Step 3 — 8 min
Chicken tenderloins
Same pan, medium-high heat, small splash of oil. Pat tenderloins completely dry, salt and pepper. In — don't touch 3–4 minutes until genuinely golden. Flip, 2 minutes.

Now — reduce to medium-low. Generous knob of butter in. Sage leaves in whole — they'll crisp in the butter, smell incredible. Garlic cloves lightly smashed in. Tilt the pan slightly and spoon that sage butter over the tenderloins continuously — 1–2 minutes. This is basting. Everything goes golden and fragrant.

Out onto a plate, rest 3 minutes. The sage leaves are edible — actually the best part. Eat them. 😄
Step 4 — while chicken rests
Parmesan mash
Drain potatoes completely. Back into the warm pot, low heat, 1 minute to dry them out — this matters, watery potatoes make sad mash. Off heat. Butter in first — generous knob, let it melt. Mash. Warm milk in gradually — just enough for your texture, don't go too loose. Fage in — 2–3 tbsp, stir. Parmesan grated in — generous. Salt, pepper, taste. It should be rich, creamy, slightly tangy from the Fage.
Plate
Bring it together
Mash as the base — generous spoonful. Chicken tenderloins on top, pour any sage butter left in the pan over everything. Mushrooms and zucchini alongside. Fresh oregano torn raw over the top — don't forget it this time. 😄 Parmesan grated over everything at the end.
The whole house smelled like sage butter and roasting things. A kitchen being used properly gets into the walls a little. It becomes the smell of home. 🌿
The sage leaves crisped in butter are edible — actually the best part. Slightly bitter, nutty, incredible. Forgot the oregano at the end — didn't matter, the sage butter did all the work.
