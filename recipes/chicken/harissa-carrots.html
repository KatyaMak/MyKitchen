Harissa chicken thighs with roasted carrots

April 2, 2026  ·  oven  ·  serves 2  ·  ★★★★☆
chicken thighs — bone-in skin-on next time ✦ Harissa carrots mini sweet peppers olive oil couscous Indian-style sweet potato curry steam bag
Start here — oven on 200°C / 400°F
35–40 min before dinner
Everything goes into the oven together. The timing works itself out.
Step 1 — 35 min
Chicken and carrots into the oven
Pat chicken thighs dry. Rub generously with harissa all over — don't be shy, it mellows in the oven. Drizzle with olive oil (important without skin — protects from drying). Peel and cut carrots into similar-sized sticks. Toss carrots with olive oil and a little harissa. Arrange chicken in the center, carrots and whole mini sweet peppers around the edges — edges run hotter, perfect for caramelizing vegetables. Single layer, nothing piled.

Into the oven. Timer 35 minutes. Halfway through, check carrots — shake the pan if they look pale. Chicken is done at 74°C / 165°F. The peppers will go slightly sweet and smoky.
Step 2 — 5 min before serving
Couscous
Boil ~250ml water or chicken broth. Pour over ~150g medium couscous. Add knob of butter, pinch of salt. Cover 5 minutes. Fluff with fork. Done.
Step 3 — last 5 min
Steam bag
Indian-Style Sweet Potato Curry bag — microwave per instructions (~4–5 min). The curry sauce is completely coherent with the harissa. Warm through, done.
Plate
Bring it together
Couscous as the base. Steam bag alongside or on top. Harissa chicken and roasted carrots and peppers over everything. One cohesive warming bowl.
Rainy evening. Cava Brut paired beautifully — bubbles cut through the harissa heat perfectly. 🌧️🥂
