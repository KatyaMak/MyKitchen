Chakhokhbili — Georgian chicken braise

April 20, 2026  ·  stovetop · Dutch oven  ·  serves 2  ·  ★★★★★
chicken thighs — bone-in skin-on × 2 (~570g) fresh tomatoes × 3 medium — more is better ✦ bell peppers × 5 small onion × 1 medium garlic — 4 cloves red wine ~60–80ml (Remole Toscana ✦) chicken broth — splash if needed smoked paprika · thyme · basil · coriander · dill bay leaves · crushed red pepper flakes olive oil yellow bulgur — alongside
Step 1 — 7–8 min
Render the skin
Dry Dutch oven, medium heat, no oil. Pat thighs completely dry — this matters. Skin-side down. Do not touch. Let the fat render and skin go deeply golden — 7–8 minutes. Quiet steady sizzle is correct. Flip, 2 minutes other side. Out onto a plate. Leave all the fat in the pot.
Step 2 — 8 min
Build the base
Same pot, medium heat. Onion in — stir, soften 4 minutes until translucent and going golden at edges. Garlic in — stir 1 minute. Bell peppers in — stir 3 minutes, just starting to soften. Salt and pepper. Pinch of crushed red pepper flakes if you want warmth.
Step 3 — 5 min
Tomatoes, wine, herbs
Tomatoes in, roughly crush with a spoon as they go. All dried herbs now — thyme, basil, coriander, smoked paprika, dill, bay leaves. Pour in 60–80ml red wine — scrape the bottom, all that caramelized chicken flavor comes up. Let it bubble and reduce 4–5 minutes until it looks like a loose sauce. Add 1 tbsp olive oil, stir. If tomatoes look dry — splash of broth or water.
Step 4 — 45 min
Chicken back in
Nestle thighs skin-side UP into the sauce — skin should sit above the liquid, not submerged. Lid on. Reduce to low heat — gentle lazy bubbles only. Timer 45 minutes. Lift lid once at 30 minutes to check liquid level, add small splash if dry. Otherwise leave it alone.

At 45 min — chicken cooked through, sauce thickened and dark. If sauce still thin, lid off 5 more minutes.
10 min before serving
Bulgur
150g yellow bulgur, 300ml water, pinch of salt, knob of butter. Bring to boil, simmer covered 12 minutes. Fluff with fork. It'll be ready exactly when the chicken is.
Finish
Serve
Fish out bay leaves. Taste — adjust salt. Serve straight from Dutch oven into deep bowls over bulgur. Bread alongside for the sauce. Pour the rest of that red wine. 🍷
Remole Toscana in the pot and in the glass. Skin stuck slightly during rendering — scraped up beautifully when wine went in, became the sauce.
Next time: more tomatoes — they dissolved almost completely into the braise. That's the direction to push. 🥘
